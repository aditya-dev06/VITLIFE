VIT Life - College Lifestyle & Management Portal for VIT Bhopal

VIT Life is the premier centralized platform designed specifically for students of VIT Bhopal. It integrates academic management, campus lifestyle coordination, and student growth tools into a single responsive application.

Key Features & Student Data Pages

  • VIT Bhopal Mess Menu: Daily breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner schedules for Boys Mess (Blocks 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7) and Girls Mess (Undergrad & Postgrad). Features Special Mess, Non-Veg Days, and Veg/Jain selections updated daily.
  • Campus Events & Fests: Discover upcoming university tech fests, cultural hackathons, music shows, sports tournaments, and club workshops across VIT Bhopal.
  • Student Clubs & Recruitments: Browse technical clubs (IEEE, ACM, Coding Club, AI Club), cultural societies, dramatics, and active student recruitment schedules.
  • PYQ Hub (Past Exam Papers): Download and search past term exam papers (CAT-1, CAT-2, Mid-Term, Term End Exams) with AI Vision OCR automatic question parsing for CSE, ECE, Mechanical, and BioTech courses.
  • Student Marketplace: Buy, sell, or trade used textbooks, lab coats, calculators, engineering tools, and hostel essentials securely within the VIT Bhopal student community.
  • Opportunities Hub: Explore tech internships, software engineering placements, open-source programs (GSOC, MLH, Hackathons), and research fellowships.
  • Academic Timetable Tracker: Manage daily class slots, VTOP timetable parser, lab schedules, attendance goals, and slot calculators.
  • VIT Bhopal Campus Guide: Comprehensive student guide covering hostel rules, night mess locations, bus transportation, health center hours, and academic regulations.
